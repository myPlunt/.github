<h1 align="center">myPlunt</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A social plant-care app for Android and iOS.</b></p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://myplunt.com">myplunt.com</a></p>

---

Keep a virtual garden, follow your friends' gardens, and never forget to water again.

- 💧 **Watering reminders** and **weather alerts** for each plant's location
- 🌦️ **Visuals that follow the real weather**
- 🤝 **Plant sitting**: hand care of your plants to a friend while you're away
- 📖 **PluntDex** of regional plants
- 📰 **Feed** with stories about your friends' plants: a new leaf, a first bloom, a broken watering streak

## Repositories

- [**myPlunt**](https://github.com/myPlunt/myPlunt): the monorepo with the mobile app (Expo / React Native), the REST API (Spring Boot), the landing site and the admin dashboard.
